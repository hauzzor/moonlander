## Original request

Rebuild the deterministic tunnel from `docs/spec.md`, including the near-vertical
and variable-width behavior confirmed in
`docs/changes/2026-10-19-near-vertical-variable-width-tunnel.md`.

## Goal

Replace the old gentle, constant-width generator with the rebuilt tunnel while
keeping rendering and collision geometry consistent.

## Current behavior

The generator uses a benign sum of sines, clamps to old limits, samples walls at
fixed x intervals, and offsets walls vertically at constant width. The renderer
assumes a constant-width corridor.

## Desired behavior

- x-monotonic, single-valued centerline, never 90 degrees and never doubling
  back.
- First 120 m straight at `y = 60` with 68 m total width, including negative x.
- After the straight: normal-offset walls, local perpendicular width 50-110 m,
  at least one 80-85 degree section per 1,000 m, maximum slope 85 degrees,
  elevation within 150 m of `y = 60`, at least ±100 m and three local maxima and
  minima per 2,000 m.
- Wall sampling by maximum world-space chord length of 8 m, adaptive on steep
  sections; adjacent chunks share endpoints; each segment belongs to exactly one
  80 m chunk by midpoint.
- Renderer and camera draw the actual center, width, and wall points.
- Same seed produces the same geometry; different seeds differ.

## Non-goals

- Physics, collision, sensors, autopilot, HUD, input, or e2e changes.
- Changing progress, score, controls, or ship constants.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The first 120 m is straight at `y = 60` and 68 m wide, including negative x.
2. The centerline reaches at least one 80-85 degree section per 1,000 m and never
   exceeds 85 degrees.
3. Elevation reaches at least +100 m and -100 m within 2,000 m of the straight
   and stays within 150 m.
4. Local width stays within 50-110 m and reaches at least one ≤60 m and one
   ≥90 m section per 2,000 m.
5. Wall segments have a maximum chord of 8 m, share endpoints, and are owned by
   exactly one chunk.
6. The same seed produces identical geometry; different seeds differ.
7. The renderer draws the actual walls and local width.
8. Typecheck and focused tests pass.

## Edge cases

- Transition exactly at x=120.
- Negative x and repeated chunk queries.
- Maximum slope, minimum width, maximum bend.
- Chunk seams and adaptive sampling on steep walls.
- Camera framing at the top and bottom of large climbs.

## Original request

Split from T-0008 so the deterministic autopilot controller can be rebuilt in
parallel with the simulation integration, then benchmarked against the rebuilt
tunnel and simulation.

## Goal

Prove the autopilot meets the confirmed zero-contact benchmark on the rebuilt
simulation and expose the result through the headless demo.

## Current behavior

The old autopilot benchmark targets the old gentle tunnel. The rebuilt controller
is produced by T-0008 and the integrated simulation by T-0013.

## Desired behavior

- Run 60 seconds on seeds `1, 7, 42, 1234, 2026, 5678, 99991, 314159`.
- `nearestWallDistance > 0` every tick, hull stays 100, progress is at least
  200 m per seed, median at least 300 m, and average world-x speed at least
  4 m/s.
- A reduced-speed safety check at the 20 m eye radius remains safe.
- `npm.cmd run sim:demo` remains deterministic and finite.
- The controller is deterministic for the same observation sequence; the
  benchmark must not weaken those assertions.

## Non-goals

- Changing the controller algorithm, simulation, physics, tunnel, or sensors.
- Machine learning or training infrastructure.
- Formal collision-free guarantees.
- Game UI or e2e work.

  controller; `sim:demo` is finite and deterministic.

  above.

  `packages/sim/examples/headless-demo.ts`.
- Table-driven cases: one row per fixed seed with progress, contact, hull, and
  speed results; reduced-radius case.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The 8-seed benchmark passes with zero contact and hull 100.
2. Progress, median progress, and average world-x speed meet the confirmed
   thresholds.
3. The reduced-radius safety check passes at the 20 m eye radius.
4. `npm.cmd run sim:demo` is deterministic and finite.
5. Typecheck and focused tests pass.

## Edge cases

- Near-vertical climbs and dives.
- Minimum-width sections.
- Sharp width changes.
- Repeated same-seed runs.
- Destroyed state and autopilot off.
- Reduced radius near a wall.

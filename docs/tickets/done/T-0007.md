## Original request

Split from the original T-0007 so the pure sensor module can be rebuilt in
parallel with physics and collision, followed by T-0013 integration and T-0016
console work.

## Goal

Rebuild the deterministic eye-sensor and radius-clamping helpers from
`docs/spec.md` against the rebuilt tunnel geometry, and freeze the sensor reading
types consumed by T-0013.

## Current behavior

`packages/sim/src/sensor.ts` uses the old tunnel assumptions. The rebuilt field
and geometry are produced by T-0004.

## Desired behavior

- Eye: 360 rays at absolute world angles, index i = i degrees, 0 right,
  counter-clockwise.
- Each ray starts at the ship hull rim and reports the wall-surface distance
  within the view radius, or the radius when no wall is hit.
- Reading stores radius, tick, origin, heading, and distances.
- `clampEyeSensorRadius` clamps 20-200 and defaults non-finite input to 120.
- Direction, distance-to-start, and velocity are computed by T-0013's simulation
  integration from ship state; this ticket freezes the reading types they use.
- The frozen reading types support T-0013's observation exposure; all values are
  finite.
- Scan cadence, radius persistence, and state exposure are integrated by T-0013.
- The existing integration tests in `eye-sensor.test.ts`,
  `navigation-sensors.test.ts`, and `observation.test.ts` remain intact and are
  owned by T-0013. This ticket adds pure module tests in
  `packages/sim/test/sensor-module.test.ts`; do not delete or weaken the
  integration files.

## Non-goals

- Simulation integration, scan cadence, or radius persistence (T-0013).
- Sensor console, chart, slider, needle, or radar rendering (T-0016).
- Changing tunnel generation, physics, collision, autopilot, or controls.
- Sensor noise, occlusion beyond tunnel walls, or extra sensors.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The eye-sensor reading matches the spec.
2. Eye rays use absolute world angles and hull-rim origins.
3. Radius clamping matches the spec; scan cadence and persistence are T-0013.
4. Eye distances and radius clamping are correct and finite.
5. The frozen sensor types support the integrated observation suite in T-0013.
6. Typecheck and focused tests pass.

## Edge cases

- Ray exactly at a wall endpoint or parallel to a wall.
- Ship touching a wall.
- Radius at 20 and 200.
- No-hit radius.
- Negative x and steep sections.

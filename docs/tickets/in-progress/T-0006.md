## Original request

Split from the original T-0006 so the pure collision module can be rebuilt after
physics, in parallel with the autopilot controller, followed by T-0013
integration.

## Goal

Rebuild deterministic capsule-versus-wall collision, swept/substepped movement,
penetration push-out, bounce, and impact-speed reporting against the rebuilt
tunnel geometry.

## Current behavior

`packages/sim/src/collision.ts` targets the old field. The rebuilt field and
geometry are produced by T-0004; physics helpers by T-0005.

## Desired behavior

- Capsule versus wall segments, swept or substepped to prevent tunneling.
- Impact speed is the velocity component into the surface.
- Push out of penetration; reflect normal velocity with restitution 0.35;
  preserve tangential velocity.
- Return the highest impact speed for the simulation to apply damage.
- `nearestWallDistance` reports the nearest wall distance for the observation.
- Collision does not own hull, invulnerability, destruction, or score; T-0013
  applies those.
- Use `rotate` from `physics.ts`; do not duplicate it.
- The existing integration tests in `packages/sim/test/collision.test.ts` remain
  intact and are owned by T-0013. This ticket adds pure module tests in
  `packages/sim/test/collision-module.test.ts`; do not delete or weaken the
  integration file.

## Non-goals

- Damage formula, invulnerability, hull, destruction, or outcome (T-0013).
- Physics tuning, field generation, sensors, autopilot, or UI.
- Adding gates, obstacles, or hazards.

`nearestWallDistance(field: Field, position: Vec2, angle: number): number`.

### Test contract

- Formulas and constants: restitution 0.35; capsule half-length 15 m; ship
  radius 9 m; wall radius 2 m; `maxImpactSpeed` is the largest inward normal
  speed across substeps; `nearestWallDistance` is clamped to 200 m.
- Expected values: below-threshold wall contact reverses the normal velocity
  with restitution; tangential velocity is preserved; penetration is pushed out
  with a small slop; no tunneling at high speed; nearest-wall distance equals
  the capsule-to-wall distance minus the ship and wall radii.
- Thresholds: damage threshold 2 m/s and the damage formula are applied by
  T-0013, not this ticket; this ticket returns the impact speed.
- Test file names: `packages/sim/test/collision-module.test.ts`.
- Table-driven cases: floor and ceiling contacts; low and high normal speeds;
  shallow and steep walls; multiple contacts in one step; nearest-wall
  distances at 0, inside, and outside the sensor range.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Capsule collisions are stable and do not tunnel.
2. Bounce, penetration push-out, and tangential preservation match the spec.
3. Impact speed is reported correctly for T-0013's damage application.
4. `nearestWallDistance` is correct and clamped.
5. Typecheck and focused tests pass.

## Edge cases

- Corner and seam contacts.
- High-speed impact on steep walls.
- Multiple contacts in one step.
- Ship touching a wall.
- Maximum speed and substepping limits.

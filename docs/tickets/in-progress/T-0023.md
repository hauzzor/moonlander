## Goal

Implement Level 2 "Gravity": deterministic open 2D space with fixed planets,
inverse-square gravity, solid planet collision, planet-aware eye rays, and
max-Euclidean progress.

## Requirements

- Seeded planet generation: radius 24 m, medium density (500 m cells, 0.6),
  minimum 192 m surface gap, 300 m spawn clearance, stable sorted ids.
- Surface gravity 8.0 m/s², inverse-square smooth cutoff 480 m beyond the
  surface; additive fields; gravityScale multiplies magnitude.
- No uniform gravity/walls/ground in Level 2.
- Solid ship-planet capsule-circle collision with swept/substepped movement,
  relative velocity, existing damage/bounce/invulnerability.
- Eye rays hit planet surfaces; `nearestWallDistance` is nearest solid surface.
- Progress = max Euclidean distance from spawn; score = floor(progress).
- Expose level, planets, gravity acceleration, and neutral tunnel fields in
  state/observation.
- Keep Level 1 behavior unchanged.
- Do not implement planet drift/repulsion or the game menu.

## Test contract

Deterministic generation/spacing/spawn clearance; gravity surface/zero/
superposition/attract; ship-planet collision/damage; sensor hits; progress;
Level 1 regression; finite observations; no DOM/randomness.

## Verification

Focused sim tests, typecheck, `sim:demo`; report risks.

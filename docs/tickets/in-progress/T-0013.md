## Original request

Split from the integration parts of T-0005, T-0006, and T-0007 so the pure
physics, collision, and sensor modules can be rebuilt in parallel, followed by a
single serialized simulation integration.

## Goal

Wire the rebuilt physics, collision, and sensor modules into the deterministic
fixed-step simulation, including action handling, reset, damage, invulnerability,
outcome, score, progress, eye-sensor cadence, and observation.

## Current behavior

`packages/sim/src/simulation.ts` still contains the old integration logic for the
pre-rebuild modules. The leaf modules are rebuilt by T-0005, T-0006, and T-0007.

## Desired behavior

- Fixed step `1 / 120`, gravity 1.62 m/s² downward, and no control latency
  beyond one tick.
- Drag applies after thrust, gravity, and torque, and before position
  integration. Use `linearDragFactor`/`angularDragFactor` or the corresponding
  apply helpers from `physics.ts`; do not duplicate the drag math.
- Response targets: lateral acceleration at least 2.0 m/s², both-thruster climb
  at least 12 m/s², angular acceleration at least 0.5 rad/s², 90 degree turn
  from rest at most 2.7 s, release from 20 m/s loses at least 25% after one
  second, and free-fall terminal speed at most 5 m/s.
- Use `thrusterEffect` from `physics.ts` for thrust and torque.
- Use `advanceWithCollisions` and `nearestWallDistance` from `collision.ts`.
- Use `clampEyeSensorRadius` and `scanEyeSensor` from `sensor.ts`.
- Apply damage `max(1, round((impactSpeed - 2) * 4))` above 2 m/s, at most one
  damage event per tick using the highest impact speed.
- Grant 0.75 s invulnerability after damage; collisions still bounce during it.
- Hull starts at 100, clamps to 0..100, and hull 0 sets `destroyed` and freezes
  the simulation.
- Reset restores hull, score, progress, thrusters, outcome, and tick while
  preserving the configured eye-sensor radius.
- Scans at tick 0 and every 120 ticks; `setEyeSensorRadius` clamps 20-200 and
  applies at the next scan.
- Direction is normalized to -pi..pi; distance-to-start is Euclidean from spawn;
  velocity includes world vx, vy, speed, and direction.
- Expose the four-block sensor suite and configured radius in state and
  observation; all observation values are finite.
- Score and progress follow the confirmed spec and are deterministic.

## Non-goals

- Changing the pure physics, collision, or sensor module behavior.
- Autopilot, field generation, game UI, or controls.
- Public API changes outside the T-0003 contract.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `createSimulation` uses the rebuilt physics, collision, and sensor modules.
2. The fixed-step state sequence is deterministic for the same seed and actions.
3. Fixed step, gravity, drag order, and every response target match the spec.
4. Damage, invulnerability, hull clamp, destruction, and freeze match the spec.
5. Reset restores run state while preserving the configured eye-sensor radius.
6. Scans happen at tick 0 and every 120 ticks; observation exposes a finite suite.
7. Direction, distance-to-start, and velocity are correct and finite.
8. Score and progress are deterministic and match the spec.
9. Typecheck and focused tests pass.

## Edge cases

- Reset during flight and after destruction.
- Hull exactly at 0.
- Repeated contacts during invulnerability.
- Radius change between scans and reset while pending.
- Destroyed ship and first-second sensor data.
- Zero speed and backward flight.
- Same seed and action sequence replayed twice.

## Parallelization plan

- Independent units: simulation integration is the serialized trunk; it depends
  on all three leaf modules.
- Disjoint scopes: `simulation.ts`, `simulation.test.ts`, the integration test
  files, and `helpers.ts` are owned only by this ticket.
- Shared files / integration points: this ticket is the integration point for
  `physics.ts`, `collision.ts`, and `sensor.ts`; leaf tickets add pure module
  tests in separate files.
- Wave and dependencies: wave 3 with T-0015; depends on T-0005, T-0006, T-0007.
- Max concurrency (default 2): 2.
- Coordination / handoff notes: leaf tickets freeze internal signatures; if a
  signature must change, the lead coordinates before this ticket starts.
- Why sequential, if no split: the shared simulation trunk has one writer.

## Goal

Render Levels 2/3 open space with planets, show level-aware HUD/progress, and
add the magnitude-only gravity heat map toggle (menu + H) for all levels.

## Requirements

- Level-aware renderer selects tunnel or space; draw planets at 24 m radius,
  open backdrop, no field/walls in space.
- Level HUD name and progress metric: Level 1 x-forward; Levels 2/3 max
  Euclidean; score floor.
- Eye sensor/radar uses planet hits from T-0023; sensor console remains usable.
- Heat map samples `world.gravityAt` at low resolution, magnitude-only, no
  gravity arrows, level 1 uniform field scaled by gravity slider, levels 2/3
  planet fields; cache, toggle off stops; render reads only.
- Hooks `gravity-heatmap-toggle`, `gravity-heatmap`, `data-active`, `data-level`;
  H key and pause-menu toggle.
- Do not change sim behavior or Level 1 visuals/tests unintentionally.

## Test contract

Renderer selects level; planets visible; heat map toggle/attrs/read-only in all
levels; level progress/score; sensor console/radar; responsive layout; no
console errors; Level 1 regression.

## Verification

Game tests, local Playwright, typecheck/build; report risks.

## Parallelization plan

Wave 4 with T-0027 (sim anti-gravity); sim vs game scopes disjoint. Test-engineer
alongside. Delivery `--no-live`.

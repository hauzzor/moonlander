## Original request

Rebuild deterministic collision, damage, bounce, invulnerability, and outcome
handling from `docs/spec.md`.

## Goal

Make the capsule hull collide correctly with tunnel walls at all speeds and
produce the specified damage and destruction behavior.

## Current behavior

The existing collision implementation targets the old field. It must be rebuilt
against the new wall geometry and spec.

## Desired behavior

- Capsule versus wall segments, swept or substepped to prevent tunneling.
- Impact speed is the velocity component into the surface.
- Impacts at or below 2 m/s are harmless.
- Damage above threshold is `max(1, round((impactSpeed - 2) * 4))`.
- At most one damage event per tick, using the highest impact speed.
- Push out of penetration; reflect normal velocity with restitution 0.35;
  preserve tangential velocity.
- A damage event grants 0.75 s invulnerability; collisions still bounce but do
  no damage during it.
- Hull starts at 100 and clamps to 0..100.
- Hull 0 sets outcome `destroyed` and freezes the simulation.
- Outcomes are only `flying` and `destroyed`.

## Non-goals

- Physics tuning, field generation, sensors, autopilot, or UI redesign.
- Adding gates, obstacles, or hazards.

## Decision log

| Decision | Choice | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Collision | Swept/substepped capsule vs walls | Prevent tunneling |
| Damage | Spec formula and threshold | Frozen contract |
| Invulnerability | 0.75 s after damage | Spec |
| Outcome | Freeze on hull 0 | Spec |

## Acceptance criteria

1. Capsule collisions are stable and do not tunnel.
2. Damage, bounce, invulnerability, and destruction match the spec.
3. At most one damage event occurs per tick.
4. Hull is clamped and the destroyed state freezes.
5. Typecheck and focused tests pass.

## Edge cases

- Corner and seam contacts.
- High-speed impact on steep walls.
- Repeated contacts during invulnerability.
- Hull exactly at 0.
- Reset during or after destruction.

`packages/sim/src/collision.ts`, `simulation.ts`, and collision tests.

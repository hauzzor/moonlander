## Original request

"add more height changes to the tunnel. it should go vertically sometimes even"

This is the confirmed implementation child of `docs/changes/2026-10-19-near-vertical-variable-width-tunnel.md`. Read that brief before starting.

## Goal

Implement the confirmed near-vertical, variable-width terrain, including field generation, normal-offset wall sampling, local-width observation, autopilot retuning, rendering, and tests. The tunnel stays rightward and x-monotonic at 80-85°, with perpendicular width 50-110 m and dramatic elevation changes.

## Current behavior

- The tunnel is a gentle, constant-width, 24 m / 0.35 slope sum-of-sines centerline.
- Walls are vertical offsets at a constant 68 m total width.
- Observation reports a constant half-width and a vertical center offset.
- The autopilot and renderer assume mostly horizontal, constant-width terrain.
- T-0001 has frozen the new public contract and stubs but has not changed behavior.

## Desired behavior

Follow `docs/changes/2026-10-19-near-vertical-variable-width-tunnel.md` exactly. The key requirements:

- Keep the first 120 m straight at `y=60`, 68 m wide, including negative x.
- After the straight, use an x-monotonic seeded centerline that reaches at least one 80-85° section per 1,000 m, never exceeds 85°, stays within 150 m of `y=60`, reaches at least +100 m and -100 m within 2,000 m, and has at least three local maxima and three local minima per 2,000 m.
- Use perpendicular local width in 50-110 m, reaching at most 60 m and at least 90 m per 2,000 m; no opening below 50 m. Floor and ceiling vary independently through a seeded center bias and width; the public centerline is their midpoint.
- Use normal-offset walls with a maximum 8 m chord, shared endpoints, deterministic chunk ownership, and no self-intersection.
- Make `Observation.tunnelHalfWidth` local and `tunnelCenterOffset` perpendicular to the local tangent.
- Retune the autopilot for the new terrain. Keep the 8-seed, 60-second benchmark: zero contact, hull 100, at least 200 m progress per seed, median at least 300 m, mean world-x speed at least 4 m/s.
- Update the renderer and camera to draw the actual center, local width, and wall geometry.
- Keep controls, HUD, seeds, damage, sensors, and outcome rules unchanged. Seeds may generate new layouts.

## Non-goals

- True 90° or x-non-monotonic/backtracking paths.
- Arc-length progress or score changes.
- New controls, thrusters, hazards, gates, fuel, or difficulty ramp.
- Opening narrower than 50 m total perpendicular width.
- Preserving old seed layouts.
- Editing the frozen contract files, `docs/spec.md`, or shared interfaces without stopping and requesting a new confirmed brief.
- Editing other tickets.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The first 120 m is straight at `y=60` and 68 m wide, including negative x.
2. After x=120, the centerline reaches at least one 80-85° section per 1,000 m and never exceeds 85°.
3. The centerline stays within 150 m of `y=60`, reaches +100 m and -100 m within 2,000 m, and has at least three local maxima and three local minima per 2,000 m.
4. Local perpendicular width stays within 50-110 m, reaches at most 60 m and at least 90 m per 2,000 m, and never drops below 50 m.
5. Floor and ceiling vary independently through a seeded center bias and width; the centerline is the local midpoint.
6. Wall segments have at most an 8 m chord, share endpoints, are owned by exactly one chunk, and do not self-intersect.
7. Collision remains swept and preserves bounce, damage, invulnerability, and outcomes.
8. The eye sensor remains 360 finite rim distances and detects steep, variable walls.
9. `Observation.tunnelHalfWidth` is local and `tunnelCenterOffset` is perpendicular; all values remain finite.
10. The autopilot passes 8 seeds x 60 s with zero contact, hull 100, at least 200 m progress per seed, median at least 300 m, and mean world-x speed at least 4 m/s.
11. The game renders the actual center, width, and walls; the camera follows through steep and variable-width sections.
12. Controls, HUD, seeds, sensors, rear thruster, autopilot toggle, and destruction behavior remain intact.
13. Same-seed determinism and different-seed variation hold; the simulation remains DOM-free and uses no `Math.random`.
14. `npm.cmd run verify`, delivery, the live asset wait, and the live Playwright suite pass.

## Edge cases

- x=120 transition; negative x; maximum slope; minimum width; maximum bend.
- Width and center-bias extrema at different x positions.
- Segment ownership at chunk seams with normal-offset walls.
- High-speed impact on a near-vertical wall.
- Eye rays parallel and perpendicular to steep walls.
- Autopilot at a near-vertical climb, a width pinch, and reset.
- Camera framing at the highest and lowest climbs.
- Renderer and collision using identical wall geometry.

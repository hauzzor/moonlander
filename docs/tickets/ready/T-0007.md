## Original request

Rebuild the eye, direction, distance-to-start, and velocity sensors from
`docs/spec.md`, and the corresponding sensor console.

## Goal

Provide a deterministic four-block sensor suite for manual and autonomous flight
and render the last scan in the UI.

## Current behavior

The existing sensor implementation uses the old tunnel assumptions and the old
renderer. It must be rebuilt against the new geometry and spec.

## Desired behavior

- Eye: 360 rays at absolute world angles, index i = i degrees, 0 right,
  counter-clockwise.
- Each ray starts at the ship hull rim and reports the wall-surface distance
  within the view radius, or the radius when no wall is hit.
- Reading stores radius, tick, origin, heading, and distances.
- Scans at tick 0 and every 120 ticks; configured radius 20-200, default 120;
  `setEyeSensorRadius` applies at the next scan and persists across reset.
- Direction: normalized -pi..pi horizon angle, signed degrees in the UI.
- Distance to start: Euclidean from spawn.
- Velocity: world vx, vy, speed, direction.
- Observation exposes the suite and configured radius; all values finite.
- UI: eye chart, radius slider, scan tick, direction needle, distance to start,
  velocity block, and world-aligned radar overlay.

## Non-goals

- Changing tunnel generation, physics, collision, autopilot, or controls.
- Sensor noise, occlusion beyond tunnel walls, or extra sensors.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The four-block suite matches the spec and is exposed in state and observation.
2. Eye rays use absolute world angles and hull-rim origins.
3. Scan cadence and radius behavior match the spec.
4. Direction, distance, and velocity are correct and finite.
5. The console and radar render the last scan and use the documented hooks.
6. Typecheck and focused tests pass.

## Edge cases

- Ray exactly at a wall endpoint or parallel to a wall.
- Ship touching a wall.
- Radius at 20 and 200.
- Reset while a radius change is pending.
- Destroyed ship and first-second data.
- Responsive console overlap.

## Affected interfaces

`packages/sim/src/sensor.ts`, `simulation.ts`, game sensor console and render,
sensor and observation tests.

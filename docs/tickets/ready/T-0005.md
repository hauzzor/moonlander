## Original request

Rebuild the deterministic ship physics from `docs/spec.md`.

## Goal

Implement the rigid-body model, canted bottom thrusters, rear booster,
atmospheric drag, and fixed-step response targets.

## Current behavior

The existing simulation implements the old physics. The rebuild must be derived
from the spec rather than copied.

## Desired behavior

- Fixed step `1 / 120`, gravity `1.62 m/s²` downward.
- Mass 200 kg, length 48 m, height 18 m, inertia 43,800 kg·m².
- Left and right bottom thrusters at 1,800 N, 15 degree cant, mounts
  `{-16, -9}` and `{16, -9}`.
- Rear booster 900 N at `{-24, 0}` along local +x, no torque.
- Linear drag 0.35/s and angular drag 0.25/s, applied after thrust, gravity, and
  torque and before position integration.
- Q/W continuous while held; rear latch controlled by the game.
- Response targets: lateral acceleration at least 2.0 m/s², both-thruster climb
  at least 12 m/s², angular acceleration at least 0.5 rad/s², 90 degree turn
  from rest at most 2.7 s, release from 20 m/s loses at least 25% after one
  second, free-fall terminal speed at most 5 m/s, no control latency beyond one
  tick.

## Non-goals

- Collision, damage, sensors, autopilot, field generation, or UI.
- Changing ship constants.

## Decision log

| Decision | Choice | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Model | Rigid body with capsule hull | Spec |
| Drag order | After forces, before integration | Spec |
| Rear booster | 900 N, no torque | Spec |
| Determinism | Fixed-step, seeded | Spec |

## Acceptance criteria

1. Ship constants and thrust geometry match the spec.
2. Q/W and rear thrust produce the specified forces and torques.
3. Drag applies in the specified order and meets the response targets.
4. The same inputs produce the same state sequence.
5. Typecheck and focused tests pass.

## Edge cases

- Both bottom thrusters held together.
- Rear plus bottom thrusters.
- Release from 20 m/s.
- Free fall and terminal speed.
- Reset during flight.

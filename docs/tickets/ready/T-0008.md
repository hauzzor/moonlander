## Original request

Rebuild the deterministic autopilot from `docs/spec.md` and the confirmed
near-vertical brief.

## Goal

Provide a safety-first controller that uses the full observation and meets the
empirical zero-contact benchmark on the rebuilt tunnel.

## Current behavior

The existing autopilot is tuned for the old gentle, constant-width tunnel. The
rebuild must be derived from the spec and the new terrain.

## Desired behavior

- `createAutopilot()` returns a deterministic `Controller`.
- It uses the full observation: eye, direction, distance to start, velocity,
  tunnel center and heading, and nearest-wall clearance.
- It follows the centerline and tangent, uses the rear thruster for forward
  speed and bottom thrusters for lift and attitude, and cuts rear thrust when
  clearance is low.
- It prioritizes zero wall contact over speed.
- It is deterministic for the same observation sequence.
- The game calls it once per fixed tick when E is active; the simulation does
  not own the autopilot mode.
- Benchmark: 8 fixed seeds, 60 seconds each, `nearestWallDistance > 0` every
  tick, hull 100, at least 200 m progress per seed, median at least 300 m, and
  average world-x speed at least 4 m/s.
- A reduced-speed safety check at the 20 m eye radius remains safe.

## Non-goals

- Formal collision-free guarantees.
- Machine learning or training infrastructure.
- Changing controls, physics, tunnel, or sensors.
- Autopilot lookahead beyond the observation.

## Decision log

| Decision | Choice | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Controller | Deterministic, observation-driven | Spec |
| Safety | Zero contact over speed | Spec |
| Benchmark | 200/300/4 on 8 seeds | Confirmed near-vertical brief |
| Rear thrust | Cut when clearance is low | Safety governor |

## Acceptance criteria

1. `createAutopilot()` is deterministic and uses the full observation.
2. The controller exclusively controls all three thrusters when active.
3. The 8-seed benchmark passes with zero contact and hull 100.
4. Progress, median, and average world-x speed meet the confirmed thresholds.
5. The reduced-radius safety check passes.
6. Typecheck and focused tests pass.

## Edge cases

- Near-vertical climbs and dives.
- Minimum-width sections.
- Sharp width changes.
- Reset and repeated same-seed runs.
- Destroyed state and autopilot off.

`packages/sim/src/autopilot.ts`, `packages/sim/examples/headless-demo.ts`, and
autopilot tests.
